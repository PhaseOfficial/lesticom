## AI Training & Implementation

Lesticom is leading the AI revolution in Southern Africa. Whether you're an individual, institution, or enterprise, we offer practical AI training programs and tailored implementations to help you harness the power of intelligent automation and data-driven decision-making.

### Our Offerings
- Beginner to advanced AI courses (online & in-person)
- Corporate & government AI upskilling
- Custom AI model development (NLP, vision, analytics)
- Afrocentric AI solutions & ethical frameworks

We cover real-world use cases such as fraud detection, customer service automation, predictive analytics, and intelligent document processing. Our hands-on courses equip learners with tools like Python, TensorFlow, PyTorch, and cloud-based AI platforms.
